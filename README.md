# Music + Technology Notes

A working collection of experiments, observations, and product questions around music technology, AI, artist tools, discovery, licensing, and creator economics.

I'm not an engineer, my background is in music, product-adjacent strategy, marketing, partnerships, licensing, and artist ecosystems. I'm using this space to document what I'm learning as the next generation of music technology develops.

## Areas I'm exploring

- AI music creation and editing
- Artist participation and compensation
- Recorded music and publishing rights
- Music discovery
- Consumer behavior around generative music
- Professional and enterprise use cases
- What makes emerging music products become habits rather than novelties
